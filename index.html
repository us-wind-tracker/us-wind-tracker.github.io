What to watch

    How Clearinghouse review works

    What it covers

    The Military Aviation and Installation Assurance Siting Clearinghouse coordinates DoD review of energy project applications filed with the FAA under 49 U.S.C. 44718. 10 U.S.C. 183a(b)

    The 75-day clock

    DoD must complete a preliminary review within 75 days of receiving a proper application from Transportation. DoD counts calendar days, excluding federal holidays. 183a(c)(1); DoDI 4180.02 glossary

    Notice of presumed risk

    If the review finds an adverse impact, the applicant and the state governor are notified, and the governor gets at least 30 days to comment. The notice is preliminary, not a formal objection. 183a(c)(2)-(3)

    Mitigation

    A Military Department must concur and request a mitigation response team within 70 days of the FAA filing. Agreements are signed by the assistant secretary for energy, installations and environment, or by the under secretary for acquisition and sustainment when the developer's contribution exceeds $1.5 million. DoDI 4180.02 paras. 2.1, 2.2, 3.2

    Objecting to a project

    The Secretary may object only after fully considering mitigation and finding an unacceptable risk to national security. That finding can be delegated no lower than a deputy under secretary, and Congress must get a report within 30 days. 183a(e)

    Offshore projects

    Projects on the Outer Continental Shelf reach DoD through Interior and BOEM, under a 1983 DoD-Interior agreement and the October 2024 BOEM-DoD memorandum. The 75-day clock attaches to FAA-filed applications. DoDI 4180.02 paras. 1.1, 3.4

    Sources: 10 U.S.C. 183a and DoD Instruction 4180.02 (June 15, 2023). A summary for orientation, not legal advice.
